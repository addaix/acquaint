acquaint.facts#

Facts: the small exact things agents keep asking for, as a typed file on the record they belong to.

An optional facts.yaml in any entity folder holds one current value per field, each with the disclosure label acquaint already uses, its source and its dates:

schema: acquaint/facts/v1
facts:
  name.full: {value: "Ada Lovelace", label: clear, source: "self: \"I write it Ada Lovelace\"", recorded: 2026-11-09}
  titles.current:
    value: "Analyst"
    label: clear
    source: https://example.org/about
    recorded: 2026-11-09
    valid_from: 2023-01-01
    review_by: 2027-05-09
    status: current            # current | proposed | conflicting
    supersedes: titles.current@2025-02-01
to_confirm: ["inferred time zone"]   # inferred items awaiting the operator

Field names follow JSContact (RFC 9553) where one exists (name.full, titles.*, speakToAs.pronouns, …), so vCard or schema.org exports are mappings later. label is one of acquaint.trust.LABELS. A source follows the rules of a preference line (acquaint.records.source_problem()): a date alone is not a source.

What reads them: facts_problems() (lint), brief_lines() (the brief’s “Facts” block: clear facts that are current, never amber or red) and fact_value() (who <id> -f name.full, clear only: a fact with a stricter label is refused, since who knows nothing about its reader). A fact that fails its own lint, is duplicated, is not current or is outside its valid_from/valid_to is never shown.

>>> facts_problems("schema: acquaint/facts/v1\nfacts:\n  name.full: {value: Ada, label: clear, source: operator, recorded: 2026-11-09}\n", today="2026-11-10")
